Workflow summary

Wrap-up

This workflow takes an AI training cohort from a raw idea to a running 60-day program: define outcomes and audience, design curriculum architecture, build the daily content engine, prepare enrollment and onboarding, run structured delivery with feedback loops, assess and certify, and finally package the cohort for reuse and referrals. The heaviest lift is Stage 3 (content engine) — build a two-week buffer there before your public launch date.
