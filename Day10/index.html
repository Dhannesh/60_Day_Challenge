About Me

Teaching code.

I'm a Computer Science & Engineering faculty member and full-stack developer with over a decade of experience spanning academia, IT infrastructure, and hands-on software development. My work lives at the intersection of rigorous academic teaching and practical engineering.

As a DSA Trainer and Mentor, I specialize in making complex algorithms approachable — whether I'm coaching students on competitive programming or guiding freshers through AI and prompt engineering at my software company. I believe that the best way to learn is to build, break, and rebuild.
