How this was researched ▸

Method

Each laptop is a specific, currently-reviewed configuration (not a whole product line), chosen because it had a recent, detailed independent review with concrete measured or reported numbers. Where a source gave a range (e.g. "$999–$1,299" or "6–8 hours"), we used the figure that matched the tested configuration closest to a mainstream 16GB/512GB-1TB spec, and noted the range in the sources panel.

Score derivation

Raw data (price in USD, tested/claimed battery hours, RAM+CPU tier, review verdicts on build quality and display) was normalized to a 0–10 scale per criterion so weights could be combined mathematically. This normalization step is an editorial judgment call, not a sourced number — it's flagged below and in the ranking cards wherever a figure required interpretation rather than direct reporting.

Conflicts we had to resolve

Acer Swift Go 14 battery life varies wildly by source and configuration (Snapdragon X Plus AI variant: 15–20+ hrs claimed vs. Intel Core Ultra OLED variant: ~9–11 hrs real-world). We used the Intel Core Ultra 7 155H OLED configuration throughout, since it's the variant most directly comparable in price to the other three laptops, and used the lower, real-world-tested figure rather than manufacturer claims.
HP Pavilion 15 battery figures ranged from 6h05m to 7h58m depending on test methodology and model year (15-eg0025nr vs. 15-eg0000 vs. 15-eg2025nr). We used the more conservative, real-world web-browsing figure (~6.5 hrs) from independent lab testing rather than HP's own "8+ hour" marketing claim.
Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 3 spans several sub-variants (3i Intel, 3x Snapdragon) with very different battery profiles. We used the Intel Core 5 210H "3i" configuration (PCWorld/Techlicious tested) since it's the closest price/spec match to the other three laptops; the Snapdragon 3x variant gets dramatically better battery life but is a different chip architecture with app-compatibility trade-offs.

What is and isn't a verified fact

Prices, battery-test minutes, CPU/RAM/storage specs, and direct reviewer quotes/verdicts are sourced and cited. The 0–10 scores shown in the mini-bars are our normalization of that sourced data for comparison purposes — treat them as a relative summary, not an independent measurement. Any placeholder or estimated figure is explicitly flagged with an estimate tag next to it.
